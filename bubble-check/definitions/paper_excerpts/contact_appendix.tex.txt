2180: \paragraph{Step 1: multiplying the thetas out.}
2181: For each edge of one bundle, write the SK pair of endpoint products as in \eqref{eq:sk-propagators},
2182: $\alpha_e$ for the coefficient of $\theta(\Delta)$ and $\beta_e$ for that of $\theta(-\Delta)$. At
2183: the level of the $h$ blocks the two are the two orderings of the Hankel branches,
2184: \begin{equation}
2185:   \alpha_e = h^{(1)}_{u_e}\,h^{(2)}_{v_e},
2186:   \qquad
2187:   \beta_e = h^{(2)}_{u_e}\,h^{(1)}_{v_e},
2188:   \label{eq:bundle-alphabeta}
2189: \end{equation}
2190: where the orders, states and arguments are those of \eqref{eq:h-definition}; $G^{--}$ interchanges
2191: the two names, and this is the only place the sign $\sigma_e$ of the bundle enters. The product of
2192: the bundle is a product of $n$ binomials in the two functions $\theta(\Delta)$ and
2193: $\theta(-\Delta)$. Expanding naively gives $2^n$ terms, but inside one bundle $\theta(\Delta)$ is
2194: the \emph{same} function for every factor, so idempotency $\theta(\Delta)^2=\theta(\Delta)$ makes
2195: all powers of one argument collapse to that argument, and orthogonality
2196: $\theta(\Delta)\theta(-\Delta)=0$ annihilates every term containing both arguments. Exactly the two
2197: terms of \eqref{eq:bundle-two-terms} survive. The factor $2^n$ thus never appears within a bundle:
2198: it counts the events of $n$ lines with pairwise different theta arguments, which is the case of $n$
2199: different bundles, and those are enumerated bundle by bundle.

2201: \paragraph{Step 2: one theta gives one delta.}
2202: Differentiating the two-term form with respect to the single variable $\Delta$ gives
2203: \begin{equation}
2204:   \partial_\Delta\!\left[\theta(\Delta)\prod_{e\in\cB}\alpha_e
2205:   +\theta(-\Delta)\prod_{e\in\cB}\beta_e\right]
2206:   =\delta(\Delta)\left(\prod_{e\in\cB}\alpha_e-\prod_{e\in\cB}\beta_e\right),
2207:   \label{eq:bundle-delta}
2208: \end{equation}
2209: because $\partial_\Delta\theta(\Delta)=\delta(\Delta)$ and
2210: $\partial_\Delta\theta(-\Delta)=-\delta(\Delta)$. Three consequences are worth stating explicitly.
2211: First, the bundle produces \emph{one} distribution, not $n$ of them: the whole contact information
2212: of the bundle is concentrated in the single coefficient
2213: $\prod_e\alpha_e-\prod_e\beta_e$, which is antisymmetric under
2214: $\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta$. Second, that one $\delta(\Delta)$ forces the two component times to
2215: coincide \emph{once}, so the components $U$ and $V$ merge once, independently of how many factors
2216: $\mathsf d_e$ a term of the coefficient contains; this is what fixes the merge count in
2217: \eqref{eq:base-power}. Third, for $G^{--}$ the interchange of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ turns
2218: \eqref{eq:bundle-delta} into its negative, which reproduces the single sign $\sigma_e$ of
2219: \eqref{eq:contact-weight} for a whole bundle.
2220: 
2221: \paragraph{Step 3: telescoping the antisymmetric difference.}
2222: Order the edges of the bundle as $1,\dots,n$ and abbreviate $\alpha_e=A_e$, $\beta_e=B_e$. Inserting
2223: and subtracting successive partial products gives the telescoping identity
2224: \begin{equation}
2225: \begin{split}
2226:   \prod_{e=1}^{n}A_e-\prod_{e=1}^{n}B_e
2227:   &=\sum_{k=1}^{n}\left(\prod_{e\leq k}A_e\prod_{e>k}B_e
2228:   -\prod_{e< k}A_e\prod_{e\geq k}B_e\right)\\
2229:   &=\sum_{k=1}^{n}D_k\prod_{e<k}A_e\prod_{e>k}B_e ,
2230: \end{split}
2231:   \label{eq:bundle-telescope}
2232: \end{equation}
2233: whose intermediate sum collapses because the $k$-th second summand and the $(k{+}1)$-th first
2234: summand are identical, while the first summand of $k=1$ and the last summand of $k=n$ supply the two
2235: products of \eqref{eq:bundle-delta}. Each term of \eqref{eq:bundle-telescope} contains exactly one
2236: difference factor $D_e=A_e-B_e$.
2237: 
2238: \paragraph{Step 4: collecting by subsets and the parity.}
2239: Introduce, as in \eqref{eq:bundle-odd-subsets}, the difference and the mean of the two endpoint
2240: products, $\mathsf d_e=D_e=A_e-B_e$ and $\mathsf m_e=(A_e+B_e)/2$, equivalently
2241: $A_e=\mathsf m_e+\mathsf d_e/2$ and $B_e=\mathsf m_e-\mathsf d_e/2$, and expand the spectators of
2242: \eqref{eq:bundle-telescope} in this basis. In the $k$-th term the edge $k$ supplies the explicit
2243: $\mathsf d_k$; an edge $e\in S$ with $e<k$ contributes $+\mathsf d_e/2$, an edge $e\in S$ with $e>k$
2244: contributes $-\mathsf d_e/2$, and every edge outside $S$ contributes $\mathsf m_e$. So the
2245: coefficient of the monomial $\prod_{e\in S}\mathsf d_e\prod_{e\notin S}\mathsf m_e$ is
2246: \begin{equation}
2247:   c_S = 2^{1-|S|}\sum_{k\in S}(-1)^{\#\{e\in S:\,e>k\}} .
2248:   \label{eq:bundle-coefficient}
2249: \end{equation}
2250: Writing $S=\{i_1<\dots<i_m\}$ with $m=|S|$, the exponent for $k=i_j$ is $m-j$, hence
2251: \begin{equation}
2252:   \sum_{j=1}^{m}(-1)^{m-j}=\sum_{r=0}^{m-1}(-1)^{r}
2253:   =\begin{cases}1,&m\ \text{odd},\\ 0,&m\ \text{even},\end{cases}
2254:   \qquad
2255:   c_S=\bigl(1-(-1)^{|S|}\bigr)2^{-|S|},
2256:   \label{eq:bundle-parity}
2257: \end{equation}
2258: which is \eqref{eq:bundle-odd-subsets}: only odd subsets survive, each with the pure numerical
2259: weight $2^{1-|S|}$. The empty set cannot occur, since every telescoping term carries at least the
2260: explicit $\mathsf d_k$, in agreement with $c_\varnothing=0$. The same parity follows from symmetry
2261: without any enumeration: the left-hand side of \eqref{eq:bundle-telescope} changes sign under
2262: $\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta$, in which $\mathsf d_e$ is odd and $\mathsf m_e$ even, so a monomial
2263: with an even number of $\mathsf d$ factors must cancel; the explicit computation
2264: \eqref{eq:bundle-coefficient} only fixes the remaining coefficients.

2266: \paragraph{Events, weights and the bookkeeping.}
2267: Each non-empty odd subset $S$ of one bundle is one simultaneous contact event. Its injection matrix
2268: $C^{(v,1)}_{st}$ of \eqref{eq:contact-injection} is the tensor product of the local contact
2269: selectors of the edges in $S$ --- $|1\rangle_e$ on a massless shared slot, the complementary-bit
2270: selector of \eqref{eq:R-two-vertex} on a pair of massive endpoints --- multiplied by $2^{1-|S|}$ and
2271: by the spectator factors $\mathsf m_e$ of the unselected bundle edges, then embedded in the global
2272: bit order with all spectator bits of other edges preserved. The collected form
2273: \eqref{eq:bundle-odd-subsets}, not the term-by-term form \eqref{eq:bundle-telescope}, is what the
2274: program enumerates: the $k$-th telescoping term stands for ``contract only the $k$-th line,'' but
2275: after the spectators are expanded the same subset $S$ is reached from several $k$, so an
2276: enumeration over $k$ would generate the same sector transition more than once. After collecting,
2277: each event appears exactly once with a numerical weight, and the deduplication key of the reduction
2278: is the triple (parent sector, child sector, set of selected line identifiers), which is finite
2279: because every event strictly reduces the number of current components.
2280: 
2281: \paragraph{Child base power and coincident unselected edges.}
2282: For a component $C$ produced by such events the base power is \eqref{eq:base-power},
2283: $A_{C,s}=\sum_{v\in C}\nu_{0,v}+\sum_{e\in E_s(C)}r_e+|C|-1$. The three terms have three different
2284: origins and should not be conflated: the vertex powers add because the merged integrand is a
2285: product, each contracted edge adds its own raw contact power $r_e$ because the corresponding
2286: $\delta$ brings one factor $(-\tau)^{r_e}$, and the last term counts \emph{merges of components},
2287: which is $|C|-1$ for any component of $|C|$ vertices and does not grow with the number of bundle
2288: edges contracted at once. Since \eqref{eq:base-power} depends only on the final component and on
2289: the contracted set, and not on the order or the clustering of the events, different legal event
2290: sequences that reach the same sector give the same child base power; the reduction is confluent at
2291: the level of the powers.
2292: 
2293: An unselected edge whose two ends fall into one component after a bundle event must also be reduced
2294: at equal times. For a massless full edge the shared slot then contributes only its symmetric state,
2295: because the two Wightman combinations coincide at coincident times, so the odd shared state of the
2296: edge gives zero; for a massive full edge the two single-derivative endpoint states become
2297: identified, $10\sim01$, which is the equal-time canonical quotient. Both cases are implemented the
2298: same way: the matrices are first built in the raw binary space of all endpoint and shared slots and
2299: then retracted to the true sector masters by the constant maps of
2300: \eqref{eq:SP-massless}, $P_s\,M^{\rm raw}\,S_s$, so the equal-time quotients require no further
2301: computation and no dense inversion.
