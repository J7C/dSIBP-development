2038: \paragraph{Bubble time kernel at fixed loop momentum}
2039: 
2040: The second example has two time vertices joined by two massive propagators
2041: (figure~\ref{fig:bubble-diagram}).
2042: We evaluate the integrals over the two vertex times while holding the internal
2043: momenta fixed. We do not integrate over the spatial loop momentum.
2044: \begin{figure}[H]
2045:   \centering
2046:   \includegraphics[width=0.90\textwidth]{figures/bubble1.png}
2047:   \caption{Bubble time kernel with external energy sums $P_1,P_2$ and
2048:   fixed internal momentum magnitudes $x_1,x_2$.}
2049:   \label{fig:bubble-diagram}
2050: \end{figure}
2051: 
2052: Let $\tau_1,\tau_2$ be the vertex times, $P_1,P_2$ the external energy sums,
2053: and $x_1,x_2$ the internal momentum magnitudes.
2054: The Hankel orders of the two internal lines are $\nu_{B1},\nu_{B2}$.
2055: Using the line kernels in \eqref{eq:application-line-kernels}, the Top masters are
2056: \begin{equation}
2057:  \begin{aligned}
2058:  J^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{abcd}
2059:  ={}&\int_{-\infty}^{0}d\tau_1\,d\tau_2\,
2060:  (-\tau_1)^{p_1}(-\tau_2)^{p_2}
2061:  e^{\ii(\sigma_1P_1\tau_1+\sigma_2P_2\tau_2)}\\
2062:  &\times\mathcal G^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{\nu_{B1};ab}(x_1;\tau_1,\tau_2)
2063:  \mathcal G^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{\nu_{B2};cd}(x_2;\tau_1,\tau_2).
2064:  \end{aligned}
2065:  \label{eq:bubble-indexed-masters}
2066: \end{equation}
2067: Here $a,b,c,d\in\{0,1\}$. The indices $a,b$ label the two endpoints of line 1 at vertices 1 and 2;
2068: $c,d$ label those of line 2 in the same vertex order.
2069: Here $p_1,p_2$ are the effective time powers after absorbing the mode
2070: prefactors, unlike the bare powers used in \eqref{eq:tree-time-power-map}.
2071: The contour signs are $\sigma_1,\sigma_2=\pm1$.
2072: We denote positive and negative signs by $p$ and $m$, respectively;
2073: these labels are unrelated to the time powers $p_1,p_2$.
2074: The four contour assignments are $pp$, $pm$, $mp$ and $mm$.
2075: In MadStree v1.0, assignments with equal contour signs have 22 masters after the
2076: exact state projection. Assignments with opposite contour signs have only the Top sector, with 16 masters.
2077: For each assignment, the projection onto the Top sector is
2078: $\bm J_{\sigma_1\sigma_2}
2079: =(J^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{0000},J^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{0001},\ldots,
2080: J^{\sigma_1\sigma_2}_{1111})^{\mathsf T}$.
2081: The vertex factors $(\ii\sigma_1)(\ii\sigma_2)$ give
2082: \begin{equation}
2083:   \bm K_{\mathrm{SK}}=-\bm J_{pp}+\bm J_{pm}+\bm J_{mp}-\bm J_{mm}.
2084:   \label{eq:bubble-sk}
2085: \end{equation}
2086: We use $k_s=x_1$ as the momentum unit and vary the dimensionless energy
2087: $\rho=P_1/k_s=P_2/k_s$. The parameter values in
2088: \eqref{eq:bubble-indexed-masters} are
2089: \begin{equation}
2090:  \begin{aligned}
2091:  (k_s,x_1,x_2)&=(1,1,\sqrt2),\qquad P_1=P_2=\rho,\\
2092:  (\nu_{B1},\nu_{B2})&=(\ii/4,\ii/4),\qquad
2093:  p_1=p_2=1+\ii/2.
2094:  \end{aligned}
2095:  \label{eq:bubble-parameters}
2096: \end{equation}
2097: The normalised Top $0000$ component is
2098: \begin{equation}
2099:   \widehat{\mathcal F}_{\ii/4}(\rho)
2100:   =\frac{[\bm K_{\mathrm{SK}}(\rho)]_{0000}}
2101:   {[\bm K_{\mathrm{SK}}(10)]_{0000}}.
2102:   \label{eq:bubble-normalised}
2103: \end{equation}
2104: The four zeros select the undifferentiated states at the four endpoints of the massive lines.
