962: Write the bundle as, per edge $e$, an ordered pair of endpoint products $\alpha_e$ (coefficient of
963: $\theta(\Delta)$) and $\beta_e$ (coefficient of $\theta(-\Delta)$).
964: \begin{enumerate}
965:   \item \emph{Multiply the thetas out first.} Since inside a bundle all thetas are the same
966:         function, $\theta(\Delta)^2=\theta(\Delta)$ and $\theta(\Delta)\theta(-\Delta)=0$, the
967:         product of $|\cB|$ lines collapses to \emph{two} terms rather than $2^{|\cB|}$,
968:         \begin{equation}
969:           \prod_{e\in\cB}\Big(\theta(\Delta)\alpha_e+\theta(-\Delta)\beta_e\Big)
970:           =\theta(\Delta)\prod_{e\in\cB}\alpha_e
971:           +\theta(-\Delta)\prod_{e\in\cB}\beta_e .
972:           \label{eq:bundle-two-terms}
973:         \end{equation}
974:   \item \emph{One theta, one delta.} Differentiating the shared theta yields a single
975:         $\delta(\Delta)$ whose coefficient is the antisymmetric difference under
976:         $\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta$,
977:         $\partial_\Delta\big[\theta(\Delta)\prod\alpha_e+\theta(-\Delta)\prod\beta_e\big]
978:         =\delta(\Delta)\big(\prod_e\alpha_e-\prod_e\beta_e\big)$, so all contact information of
979:         the bundle is concentrated in one distribution and the endpoints are forced to coincide
980:         only once.
981:   \item \emph{Split and change basis.} Telescoping the antisymmetric difference and then collecting
982:         in $\mathsf d_e=\alpha_e-\beta_e$ and $\mathsf m_e=(\alpha_e+\beta_e)/2$ leaves only terms
983:         with an odd number of $\mathsf d$ factors, each with a numerical coefficient,
984:         \begin{equation}
985:           \prod_{e\in\cB}\alpha_e-\prod_{e\in\cB}\beta_e
986:           =\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\neq S\subseteq\cB\\|S|\ \mathrm{odd}}}
987:           2^{1-|S|}\prod_{e\in S}\mathsf d_e\prod_{e\notin S}\mathsf m_e .
988:           \label{eq:bundle-odd-subsets}
989:         \end{equation}
990:   \item \emph{Read off the events.} Every non-empty odd subset $S$ is one simultaneous contact
991:         event: the edges of $S$ are contracted at once, their endpoints merge once, the component
992:         count drops by one, and the base power of the merged component follows
993:         \eqref{eq:base-power} with $|C|-1$ merge terms. The injection matrix
994:         $C^{(v,1)}_{st}$ of \eqref{eq:contact-injection} for such an event is the tensor product of
995:         the local selectors of the edges in $S$, multiplied by $2^{1-|S|}$ and by the spectator
996:         factors $\mathsf m_e$.
997: \end{enumerate}
998: The collected form \eqref{eq:bundle-odd-subsets} rather than the term-by-term telescoping is what
999: the program uses, because a term-by-term enumeration would count the same sector transition
1000: several times; the argument, the parity proof and the treatment of unselected edges whose endpoints
1001: become coincident are given in App.~\ref{app:theta}.
